# Product Steering

- Internal Partnership CRM for AIESEC in PU's BD subdivision.
- The five official ESSM stages are Prospecting → Sales Process → Close Deal → Partnership Implementation → Post Survey.
- Use ten ESSM industry categories and ESSM Project Support; never replace them with B2B/B2C/MSME verticals.
- Partnership Alignment is human-entered: Values, Vision, Current Focus and Program, each integer 1–5, plus Proceed.
- Rate Card and Merchandise Tracker are first-class modules. Proposal amounts are calculated snapshots; actual financial/in-kind receipts are separate.
- Monthly partner newsletter and finished-program report link are mandatory ESSM items; visit, invitation, greeting are optional. Track official BD scorecard link/status.
- TL BD and LCVP PD share one CRM Lead permission set; LoA self-approval is always prohibited.
- AI drafts and summarizes only. Humans own scores, proceed decisions, replies/outcomes, SnD, final MoM, approval, signing and external messages.
- See `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/ESSM_MAPPING.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` for full rules.
