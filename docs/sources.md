This repo pack was prepared from user-provided materials and decisions: the ESSM BD Guideline of AIESEC in Indonesia (last updated February 2026), Partnership Guidance of AIESEC in Indonesia (last updated February 2026), the ESSM Tools 2026 workbook, the prior PRD v1.1, and the 13-page `Partnership_CRM_Diagrams_FINAL_EDGE_ANCHORED.drawio.xml`. Use the live workbook locally during the import/export mapping spike to confirm exact sheet names, columns, formulae and dropdown values. This document does not claim those cell positions have been verified programmatically in the handover pack.
